# Content notes for the teacher

Points to check against your own teaching materials before students use the tool.

## Differences found in the source files

These were spotted while building the revision bank. The tool uses the corrected version in each case. Your original files were not changed.

| Where | What the file says | What the tool uses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lesson deck, display screen equipment | 1997 | Health and Safety (Display Screen Equipment) Regulations 1992, amended 2002 |
| Lesson deck, interception law | Interception of Communications Act 1986 | The Interception of Communications Act 1985, replaced by RIPA 2000 (and later the Investigatory Powers Act 2016) |
| Workbook, data protection | Data Protection Act 1998 | UK GDPR with the Data Protection Act 2018 |
| Quay Cycles exemplar answers | 42 staff | 38 staff, as the brief says |
| Quay Cycles practice paper | "Technology" in the faculty name | "Technologies" |

Please check the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 against the current specification and your exams officer. The tool mentions it only lightly, because its effect on the content of this unit is not yet settled in the materials supplied.

## Scenario facts used from the Quay Cycles brief

Four shops (Exeter, Plymouth, Totnes, Barnstaple) plus a planned fifth in Truro, 38 staff, a nine year old server with a USB stick backup in the same room, a freelancer called Sam paid by the day, Dan as the only technician two days a week, a set up budget of £30,000 then no more than £2,000 a month. If you change the brief next year, update `U14.scenarios.quay` and the Quay tasks in `js/data/practice.js` together.

## Series where evidence is limited

* January 2020: only the examiner report was supplied, so the walkthrough is shorter and says so.
* January 2024: only the paper was supplied, with no examiner report.
* Grade boundaries are not given in the tool because they change each series. The Exam coach says to check with the tutor.

## Assessment integrity

The Part A research phase and the Part B task are independent assessments. The Home page tells students not to use the tool during the live window if the tutor says so, and the lock in `js/config.js` can switch the tool off for set dates. Past paper walkthroughs deliberately describe the scenario and the lessons, and do not offer worked answers to live set tasks.
